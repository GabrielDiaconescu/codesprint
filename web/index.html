Subscriptions and purchases

Purchases are made through the App Store or Google Play, and they are managed there. Deleting your CodeSprint account does not cancel a subscription.

  • Cancel on iPhone or iPad: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → CodeSprint.
  • Cancel on Android: Google Play → your profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → CodeSprint.
  • Restore a purchase on a new phone: sign in with the same account, then Profile → Subscription → Restore purchases.
  • Refunds are decided by Apple or Google under their own policies. Request one from the store you paid.

Your account

  • Forgot your password: on the sign-in screen, tap “Forgot your password?”.
  • A copy of your data: Profile → Your data → Export my data.
  • Delete your account: Profile → Your data → Delete account, or see how to delete your account.
