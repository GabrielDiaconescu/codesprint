On your device

Under Romanian Law no. 506/2004, storing or reading information on your phone needs your consent unless it is strictly necessary for the service you asked for. The app stores your sign-in session, your settings (theme, language, vibration, reminders) and a content cache, which are necessary. It uses device storage for analytics and personalised advertising only with your consent. Crash reporting keeps unsent reports on the device, and update checks keep a random installation identifier; we consider both necessary to keep the app secure and working [LAWYER TO CONFIRM — otherwise behind consent].

4. Where your data comes from

From you, and from how you use the app. Your plan and purchase events come from Apple or Google through RevenueCat; your advertising choice comes from Google's consent form.

5. Children and teenagers

  • CodeSprint is for people aged 13 and over. If you tell us you are younger, no account is created and the one started is deleted immediately.
  • In Romania, consent to data processing for online services counts only from 16 (art. 8 GDPR). So if you are under 16 we never ask for it: analytics stays off, ads are never personalised and Google is told you are under the age of consent, and you are hidden from the public leaderboard by default.
  • A parent or guardian can ask us for a copy of their child's data, or to delete the account, at [PRIVACY EMAIL].

7. How long we keep it

  • Your account and everything in it: until you delete it.
  • Guest accounts not used for 30 days are deleted automatically.
  • Sessions on a device: until you sign out, or the session expires.
  • When an account is deleted, the purchase events we received are emptied of their contents; only an event reference remains, so the same event can never be applied twice.
  • Database backups: 7 days. Server logs: 7 days.
  • Crash reports: up to 90 days. Analytics: 12 months.
  • Support messages: until your request is resolved, and for 12 months after, unless we need them longer to establish, exercise or defend a legal claim.
  • Apple and Google keep their own purchase records, as the law requires of them.

8. Your rights

  • Access and portability — a copy of your data, in a machine-readable file: Profile → Your data → Export my data.
  • Rectification — edit your profile in the app, or write to us.
  • Erasure — Profile → Your data → Delete account, or ask us.
  • Withdraw consent for analytics or personalised ads at any time, in Profile → Privacy. It does not affect what happened before.
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interests, or ask us to restrict it.
  • Complain to the Romanian data protection authority, ANSPDCP — B-dul G-ral. Gheorghe Magheru 28-30, sector 1, București, dataprotection.ro — or to the authority where you live or work.

Write to [PRIVACY EMAIL] from the address your account uses, so we can be sure it is you. We answer free of charge, within one month — extendable by two more months for complex requests, in which case we tell you within the first month.

9. Security

All traffic is encrypted. Every learner can reach only their own data, enforced in the database itself; answers are graded on our servers; passwords are stored only as hashes; and server keys never ship inside the app.

10. Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app before the change takes effect. The date at the top shows the current version.
