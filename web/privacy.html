  • You can use CodeSprint as a guest, without an email address.

Children and teenagers

  • CodeSprint is for people aged 13 and over. If you tell us you are younger, no account is created and the one started is deleted immediately.
  • If you are under 16: analytics stays off, ads are never personalised, and you are hidden from the public leaderboard by default.
