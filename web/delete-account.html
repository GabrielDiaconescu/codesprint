Delete your CodeSprint account

CodeSprint: Learn to Code, by [CONTROLLER NAME]

In the app — immediate

  1. Open CodeSprint and go to Profile.
  2. Under Your data, tap Delete account.
  3. Read what will be deleted, and confirm.

Want a copy first? In the same place, tap Export my data.

Without the app

Email [SUPPORT EMAIL] from the address your account uses, with the subject “Delete my account”. We delete it without undue delay, and at the latest within one month, and confirm by email. A guest account has no email address, so it can only be deleted from the app — or it deletes itself after 30 days without use.

What is deleted

  • Your account, email address, password and sign-in sessions on every device.
  • Your profile, year of birth and onboarding answers.
  • All learning data: answers, XP, streaks, progress, achievements, challenge and exam results, and your place on the leaderboard.
  • Your consent choices, and the reports you filed.
  • Your customer record at our subscription provider.

What is kept, and for how long

  • A reference to each purchase event we received, emptied of its contents, so the same event can never be applied twice.
  • Database backups and server logs, which are overwritten within 7 days.
  • Crash reports (up to 90 days) and, if you had allowed analytics, usage statistics (12 months). They carry only a pseudonymous id that no longer matches any account, and are deleted when those periods end.
  • Apple and Google keep their own records of your purchases.
Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it first in your App Store or Google Play settings — here is how — or it will keep renewing.
