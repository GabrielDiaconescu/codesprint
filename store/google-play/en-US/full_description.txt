Learn to Code. 5 Minutes a Day. CodeSprint teaches Python in short, interactive sessions you can finish on a bus ride — and gives you a reason to come back tomorrow.

LEARN BY DOING
• Bite-sized lessons: a short explanation, a real code example, then questions.
• Eight kinds of exercise: multiple choice, true or false, predict the output, fill in the blank, put lines of code in order, find the bug, match concepts, and pick every right answer.
• Every answer is explained, right or wrong.

A CLEAR PATH THROUGH PYTHON
17 modules of Python Fundamentals take you from print() and variables through conditions, loops, lists, functions, dictionaries, exceptions and files to your first classes and simple problem solving.

PRACTISE WHAT YOU LEARNED

• A Python Fundamentals exam, with your results broken down by skill.
